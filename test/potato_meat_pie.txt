Potato Meat Pie

A savory pie with a potato dough crust filled with seasoned minced meat and topped with shredded mozzarella cheese.

Prep: 30m Cook: 30m Total: 1h
Serves: 6

Ingredients:
2 large potatoes (450g)
1 & 1/2 cups all purpose flour
1 cup fine semolina
2 eggs
1 tbsp salt
1 tbsp mixed spices
1 tbsp baking powder
2 tbsp melted butter
0.5 kg minced meat
1 tbsp butter + oil
1 chopped onion
2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
1 tsp paprika
Salt & pepper
Shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions:
1\. Prepare the dough by boiling and mashing the potatoes, then mixing with flour, semolina, eggs, salt, spices, baking powder, and melted butter to form a dough.
2\. Prepare the meat filling by sautéing chopped onions in butter and oil, then adding minced meat, Worcestershire sauce, paprika, salt, and pepper, cooking until well combined.
3\. Roll out the potato dough and line a baking dish with it.
4\. Spread the cooked meat filling over the dough.
5\. Top with shredded mozzarella cheese.
6\. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (356°F) until golden brown and cooked through.
